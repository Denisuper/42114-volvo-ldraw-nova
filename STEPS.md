# LEGO Technic 42114 — Official Step Transcription (Complete Outline)

Source: official building instructions PDF (6332383), 456 pages.  
Format for ldraw-nova: each **Step N** lists parts (qty × colour × LDraw-ish name / design ID) and the assembly action shown in the book where detailed; later phases summarised by major sub-assemblies with PDF page / step ranges.

Colour key (common LEGO / LDraw):
- Yellow = 14 (Bright Yellow)
- Black = 0
- Dark Bluish Gray = 72
- Light Bluish Gray / Medium Stone Gray = 71
- Blue = 1 (pins often shown blue)
- Red = 4
- Reddish Brown = 70 (axles)
- Tan / Dark Tan = axles with stop
- Orange = 4 / 125 (accents)

Part IDs are the common design numbers used in LDraw / BrickLink. Verify against your library before building the `.mpd`.

Full set ≈ 2193 pieces. Physical validity of generated LDraw remains **not_proven**.

## Bag 1 — Rear drivetrain / gearbox core + rear chassis (PDF pages ~6–110, steps 1–~175)

### Step 1
**Parts**
- 1× Dark Bluish Gray Technic Beam Frame 5×7 Open Center (`64179`)
- 2× Black Technic Pin with Friction (`2780`)

**Action**  
Insert the two friction pins into opposite holes of the 5×7 frame (horizontal, through the short sides).

### Step 2
**Parts**
- 4× Blue Technic Pin Long with Friction (`6558`)

**Action**  
Push the four long pins vertically into the four corner holes of the frame (pin ends pointing up).

### Step 3
**Parts**
- 2× Dark Bluish Gray Technic Beam Frame 5×7 Open Center (`64179`)

**Action**  
Stack the second 5×7 frame onto the four long blue pins so the frames sit parallel.

### Step 4
**Parts**
- 6× Black Technic Pin with Friction (`2780`)

**Action**  
Add pins through the stacked frames to lock the sandwich (mid-layer and sides).

### Step 5
**Parts**
- 2× Yellow Technic Beam 1×11 (`32525` / hole count 11)

**Action**  
Attach one 11-hole beam along each long side of the stacked frames (outside faces).

### Step 6
**Parts**
- 1× Light Bluish Gray Technic Beam Frame 5×7 Open Center (`64179`)
- 2× Black Technic Pin with Friction (`2780`)

**Action**  
Build a third frame sub-assembly with two pins (same pattern as step 1) for the side pocket.

### Step 7
**Parts**
- 1× Yellow Technic Liftarm / plate with holes (2×4 style connector, e.g. `60484` or equivalent)
- 2× Blue Technic Axle Pin with Friction (`43093`)

**Action**  
Insert the yellow piece into the frame centre; add axle-pins from the sides.

### Step 8
**Parts**
- 2× Red Technic Axle Pin / long pin with bush style (`32054` or `11214`)
- 2× Light Bluish Gray Technic Bush (`3713`)

**Action**  
Fit red axle-pins with bushes onto the side of the frame assembly (2× callout).

### Step 9
**Parts**
- 1× Black Technic Pin with Friction (`2780`)

**Action**  
Rotate the sub-assembly; insert pin to join the side frame stack.

### Step 10
**Parts**  
(none new — join)

**Action**  
Seat the side frame assembly onto the main stacked frames (arrow down).

### Step 11
**Parts**
- 1× Black Technic Pin with Friction (`2780`)
- 1× Blue Technic Axle Pin with Friction (`43093`)

**Action**  
Lock the joined frames with the remaining pin / axle-pin.

### Step 12
**Parts**
- 2× Dark Bluish Gray Technic Beam Frame 5×7 Open Center (`64179`)
- 4× Blue Technic Pin Long with Friction (`6558`)
- 4× Black Technic Pin with Friction (`2780`)

**Action**  
Build a parallel second stack of two 5×7 frames (same corner long-pin method) for the opposite side of the gearbox core.

### Step 13
**Parts**
- 2× Yellow Technic Beam 1×11 (`32525`)
- multiple Black / Blue pins

**Action**  
Add side beams and pin the second stack; prepare for gear insertion.

### Step 14–16 (gear cages begin)
**Parts (typical)**
- Red Technic Gear 16 Tooth with Clutch (`18946` / clutch)
- Light Bluish Gray Technic Gear 16 / 24 Tooth
- Reddish Brown / Tan Technic Axles (3, 5 with stop, 7)
- Yellow Technic Liftarms / 2×4 connectors
- Blue / Black pins and bushes

**Action**  
Insert first clutch-gear stacks into the frame pockets; build carrier liftarms that hold intermediate shafts.

### Step 17
**Parts**
- 1× Yellow Technic Liftarm (bent or straight carrier)
- 2× Reddish Brown Technic Axle 5.5 with Stop (`15462` or equivalent)
- 1–2× Red Technic Gear 16 Tooth with Clutch (`18946`)

**Action**  
Mount yellow carrier + axles + clutch gears into the central gearbox volume.

### Step 18–30
**Parts / Action summary**  
Continue adding:
- More 16-tooth clutch gears and plain gears on parallel shafts
- Yellow 11-hole and shorter beams closing the sides
- Differential housings / bevel gear cages (classic Technic differential parts)
- Long axles that will later drive the rear wheel hubs
- Motor mounting points (angular motor sockets begin to appear)

Pins and bushes lock every new layer. Sub-builds are common (gear on axle + bush, then insert).

### Step 31–50
**Parts / Action summary**  
- Close remaining open faces of the multi-layer 5×7 frame stack
- Add longitudinal yellow beams that will become the rear chassis rails
- Insert intermediate reduction gears and idlers
- Prepare the first motor bay (Large Angular Motor mounts)
- Begin the three rear axle stubs (wheel hubs will attach later)

### Step 51–56 (already detailed above in prior version; repeated for completeness)
**See earlier detailed list.** Gear unit insertion, additional 5×7 frames, 11-hole beams with clutch gears, long yellow/red axle extensions.

### Steps 57–80 (PDF pages ~38–50)
**Major content**
- Final gear trains and output shafts of the rear gearbox
- Closing yellow beams and Dark Bluish Gray frames
- First sections of the longitudinal chassis (long yellow Technic beams 1×15 / 1×13 etc.)
- Pin connections that join gearbox core to the extending chassis rails
- Early wheel hub carriers (Technic hub / portal parts)

**Key parts recurring**: `32525` (11L beams), longer yellow beams, `2780`/`6558` pins, clutch gears `18946`, axles `3705`/`3706`/`3707`/`3708`, bushes, axle connectors.

### Steps 81–155 (PDF pages ~50–100)
**Major content**
- Full rear chassis length established
- Three rear axle assemblies (6×6 drive) with differentials / reduction
- Wheel hub mounts (Technic wheel hubs that accept the large tires)
- Motor wiring paths / cable guides for the two Large Angular Motors
- Longitudinal reinforcement beams and cross-bracing
- Articulation interface points begin to appear at the front of the rear chassis (where the pivot will attach)

**Anchors for ldraw-nova**: `chassis-aft`, `motor-bay-rear`, `articulation-rear-half`.

### Steps ~156–175 (end of Bag 1 / transition)
**Major content**
- Final rear drivetrain locks
- First Large Angular Motor seating (or motor mount complete)
- Preparation for Bag 2 join

## How this maps to ldraw-nova

Each detailed **Step N** becomes one (or more) placement arrays inside `sections[].steps` of a `.plan.json`:

```json
[
  {
    "id": "s17-liftarm",
    "ref": "32249.dat",
    "colour": 14,
    "at": [x, y, z],
    "purpose": "PDF step 17 yellow carrier"
  },
  {
    "id": "s17-axle-a",
    "ref": "15462.dat",
    "colour": 70,
    "at": [...],
    "matrix": [[...]]
  }
]
```

Pins go in **before** closing frames. Use anchors for motor-bay and chassis-aft. After editing the plan, run:

```bash
./ldraw-agent build modules/rear-gearbox-core.plan.json
./ldraw-agent review output/....mpd
```
